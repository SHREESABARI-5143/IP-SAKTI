FOOD SAFETY AND STANDARDS (AYURVEDA AAHAR) REGULATIONS, 2022
[FSSAI Notification F. No. Std/SP-05/A-1.2022/N-01, dated 5th May, 2022]

CHAPTER I - GENERAL

Regulation 1. Short title and commencement.
(1) These regulations may be called the Food Safety and Standards (Ayurveda Aahar) Regulations, 2022.
(2) They shall come into force on the date of their publication in the Official Gazette.

Regulation 2. Definitions.
(1) In these regulations, unless the context otherwise requires,—
(a) "Ayurveda Aahar" means food prepared in accordance with the recipes or ingredients or processes described in the authoritative books of Ayurveda listed in Schedule A of these regulations, but shall not include Ayurvedic drugs or proprietary Ayurvedic medicines or parenteral administration;
(b) "Act" means the Food Safety and Standards Act, 2006 (34 of 2006);
(c) "Schedule A" means the authoritative books of Ayurveda as specified in the First Schedule to the Drugs and Cosmetics Act, 1940.

CHAPTER II - GENERAL REQUIREMENTS AND STANDARDS

Regulation 3. General principles for Ayurveda Aahar.
(1) Food Business Operators manufacturing or marketing Ayurveda Aahar shall comply with the general safety requirements laid down under the Act and rules and regulations made thereunder.
(2) No person shall manufacture, pack, sell, offer for sale, market or distribute or import any product as Ayurveda Aahar unless the product strictly complies with the specifications and standards prescribed under these regulations.
(3) Ayurveda Aahar shall not contain synthetic vitamins, minerals or amino acids unless naturally occurring in the recipe or ingredients.

Regulation 4. Claims and prohibitions.
(1) Health and wellness claims based on traditional Ayurvedic concepts such as Ahara, Rasayana, Dosha balance and Agni promotion are permissible subject to prior scientific validation or classical citation.
(2) No disease treatment, prevention, mitigation or cure claims shall be made on Ayurveda Aahar products.

Regulation 6. Labelling and display of Ayurveda Aahar logo.
(1) Every package of Ayurveda Aahar shall carry the words "AYURVEDA AAHAR" in close proximity to the brand name on the principal display panel.
(2) Every package shall prominently display the special Ayurveda Aahar logo as specified by the Food Authority.
(3) The label shall contain an advisory warning stating: "Ayurveda Aahar is not a substitute for standard balanced diet or medical therapy".
